An app that registered an address is told right away and directly, with a saying who logged out, and clears what it holds for them. Its next login asks again, because the it relied on has gone. An app that registered no address keeps them logged in until it next asks, which may be a while. There is also a front-channel way, through hidden pages in the browser, which was not tried here.
